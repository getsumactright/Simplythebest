Meet the owner

Carlos Raymond Abreu. Just call him Ray.

Ray began his career in drywall in 1996, right after high school. After a couple of years as a laborer and detailer, he joined the Painters and Tapers Union Local 487, where his commitment to excellence and a high-quality product was refined over about 16 years. Today he's made a name for himself as a residential specialist.

Ray, owner
