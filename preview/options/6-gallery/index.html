About the artist. Call him Ray.

Carlos Raymond Abreu began his career in drywall in 1996, shortly after graduating high school. After a couple of years as a laborer and detailer, he joined the Painters and Tapers Union Local 487, where his commitment to excellence and a high-quality product was refined. Today he's a residential specialist who has made a name for himself and his company.
